When to bring Lochlann in

Senior experience where the opportunity needs it.

An unfamiliar government account, consequential pursuit, demanding partnership, or approaching startup milestone can all benefit from a dedicated senior lead. The work is scoped to complement the team and capability already in place.

01

A key pursuit needs experienced leadership.

Bring discipline to qualification, competitor analysis, customer priorities, capture choices, and the decisions ahead of the bid.

02

Your team is entering a new customer or market.

Assess the mission, stakeholders, acquisition pathways, and local or specialist partnerships that could shape a credible entry plan.

03

A partner or proposal effort needs an accountable lead.

Coordinate teaming, workshare, proposal milestones, reviews, and the people needed to carry the pursuit through.

04

An award is moving toward mobilization.

Translate commitments into a practical startup: owners, partner actions, schedule, customer milestones, and acceptance expectations.
