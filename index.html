The need-to-delivery arc

The customer hears one promise.

Account strategy, capture, pricing, teaming, and delivery are often managed by different groups. Lochlann connects those decisions so the pursuit reflects what the organization can actually approve, build, and deliver.

01

Test the opportunity

Clarify the customer’s priorities, funding and procurement path, decision makers, competitive position, and whether the work deserves investment.

02

Build the path to win

Shape requirements early, define a differentiated and compliant solution, assemble the right team, and make pursuit investments based on evidence.

03

Align the decision-makers

Bring technical, commercial, regional, partner, and program leaders together before proposal commitments harden.

04

Carry the promise into delivery

Turn proposal commitments into clear scope, schedule, cost, site requirements, named owners, acceptance criteria, and customer communication.
