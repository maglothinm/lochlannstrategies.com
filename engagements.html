Approach

Start with the decision. End with a team ready to carry it forward.

Lochlann’s work is structured around the issue in front of leadership, the accountable internal owner, and a practical handoff—not a standing layer of consulting.

Outside support should make the internal team more capable—not more dependent.

Engagement modes

Use the level of support the situation requires.

Each mode begins with a defined decision or operating problem and ends with a clear work product, accountable owner, and next-step logic.

01

Executive Assessment

A rapid, independent view when leadership needs to establish facts, test assumptions, compare options, or make a consequential decision.

  • Facts and findings
  • Decision brief
  • Recommended next steps
02

Focused Growth or Pursuit Leadership

Direct, hands-on leadership for a strategic account, market-entry effort, partner route, capture campaign, or cross-functional growth priority.

  • Growth or capture plan
  • Cross-functional team
  • Decision schedule
03

Transition or Recovery Support

Structured intervention when an award, program, partner arrangement, or operating handoff needs clearer ownership, risk control, and recovery logic.

  • Immediate risk priorities
  • Recovery plan
  • Handoff to accountable owner

Working cadence

A disciplined path from ambiguity to ownership.

The cadence is adapted to the assignment, but the underlying pattern remains consistent.

01

Get to the facts.

Review the customer, opportunity, operating constraints, existing commitments, data, and stakeholder views.

02

Decide and organize.

Frame the options, assign ownership, establish decision rights, and build the minimum useful plan.

03

Work to decisions.

Use a practical cadence of actions, reviews, risks, and executive decisions rather than activity reporting.

04

Hand it to the team.

Transfer the logic, tools, relationships, open decisions, and operating rhythm to the accountable owner.

Working principles

Specific enough to be useful. Disciplined enough to be trusted.

01

Built for the organization

The answer must work with the client’s structure, authority, data, people, and actual operating environment.

02

Specific and accountable

Recommendations are tied to decisions, owners, timing, evidence, and consequences rather than abstract best practices.

03

An honest commercial picture

Pipeline, bookings, revenue, award value, margin, cost, risk, and investment are kept distinct so leadership can act on the real economics.

04

Boundaries from the start

Scope, information controls, authority, deliverables, and handoff conditions are defined before execution begins.

The intended outcome

The client team should be stronger when the engagement ends.

That means clearer decisions, better operating discipline, and an accountable internal owner who can carry the work forward.
