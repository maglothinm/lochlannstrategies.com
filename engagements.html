Approach

Start with the decision. End with a team ready to carry it forward.

Some situations need an independent assessment. Others need hands-on leadership or a short recovery effort. In every case, the scope, owner, decisions, and handoff are clear from the start.

Outside support should make the internal team more capable—not more dependent.

Ways to engage

Match the engagement to the issue in front of you.

The right format depends on the decision, the urgency, and whether leadership needs a clear assessment, hands-on leadership, or direct intervention.

01

Executive Assessment

A focused, independent review when leadership needs a clear read on an account, market, pursuit, partner strategy, commercial choice, or delivery risk.

  • Facts and findings
  • Decision brief
  • Recommended next steps
02

Focused Account or Pursuit Leadership

Hands-on leadership for a defined account initiative or major pursuit. The sponsor, team, decision rights, review rhythm, and eventual handoff are agreed at the start.

  • Account or capture plan
  • Cross-functional team
  • Decision schedule
03

Transition or Recovery Support

A short, focused effort to steady a transition or recover a program when commitments are unclear, ownership is split, forecasts have lost credibility, partners are out of alignment, or customer confidence is slipping.

  • Immediate risk priorities
  • Recovery plan
  • Handoff to the accountable owner

How the work runs

Four steps from a hard question to an owned plan.

The sequence adapts to the situation and works within the client’s existing account, capture, regional, and delivery structure.

01

Get to the facts

Review the account, relationships, pipeline, assumptions, delivery history, decisions already made, and risks no one yet owns.

02

Decide and organize

Set the priority, test the case for action, name the executive sponsor, and bring together the people needed to move it.

03

Work to decisions

Run against clear milestones, customer actions, capture gates, commercial reviews, forecast updates, and executive decisions.

04

Hand it to the team

Leave the accountable owner with the plan, relationships, commitments, risks, and working rhythm needed to continue without losing momentum.

Working principles

Straightforward standards for the work.

01

Built for the organization

Strengthen the internal team, connect the right business lines and regions, and leave behind plans and tools people will actually use.

02

Specific and accountable

Every recommendation states what should change, who owns the next move, what evidence supports it, and when a decision is due.

03

An honest commercial picture

Keep pipeline, bookings, revenue, award value, margin, cost, and risk separate so leadership sees the opportunity as it is.

04

Boundaries from the start

Confidentiality, representation, intellectual property, and potential conflicts are addressed before work begins.

The measure

The client team should be stronger when the engagement ends.

Success means clearer ownership, better decisions, a pursuit the organization can actually execute, and a cleaner handoff into delivery.
