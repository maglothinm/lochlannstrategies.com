Approach

Structured around the account outcome—not the consulting activity.

Work begins with the decision, the internal owner, the evidence available, and the point at which responsibility must transfer into the enterprise.

Independent judgment should sharpen enterprise ownership, not replace it.

Engagement modes

Choose the intervention that matches the consequence.

The scope can range from a bounded executive assessment to hands-on leadership, but decision rights and transfer points remain explicit.

01

Executive Assessment

A bounded assessment for an account, market, pursuit, partner route, commercial decision, or delivery risk that requires independent judgment.

  • Evidence review
  • Decision memo
  • Priority actions
02

Focused Account or Pursuit Leadership

Hands-on leadership for a defined account initiative or material pursuit, with the internal sponsor, team, governance, and transition point established at the outset.

  • Account or capture plan
  • Team mobilization
  • Executive governance
03

Transition or Recovery Support

Short-duration intervention when commitments, delivery ownership, forecast quality, partner alignment, or client confidence need to be stabilized.

  • Risk triage
  • Recovery cadence
  • Controlled handoff

Operating cadence

A practical sequence from evidence to durable ownership.

The method fits within enterprise account teams, capture governance, regional ownership, and delivery organizations.

01

Establish the baseline

Clarify account maturity, relationships, pipeline quality, assumptions, decision rights, delivery history, and unresolved risk.

02

Choose and mobilize

Set priorities, define the thesis, identify the sponsor, assemble the cross-functional team, and allocate effort against evidence.

03

Govern the work

Use explicit milestones, customer actions, capture gates, commercial reviews, forecast updates, and executive decisions.

04

Transfer durable ownership

Document the plan, relationships, commitments, risks, and cadence so responsibility moves inside without losing continuity.

Operating principles

What the work must preserve.

01

Enterprise-minded

Strengthen the internal team, connect business lines and regions, and leave usable account and pursuit architecture behind.

02

Direct and accountable

Recommendations identify what changes, who owns the action, what evidence supports it, and when the decision returns.

03

Commercially grounded

Pipeline, bookings, revenue, award value, margin, cost, and risk remain distinct rather than blended into an inflated story.

04

Conflict-aware

Confidentiality, representation, intellectual property, and conflict boundaries are explicit from the beginning.

The test

Will the organization be stronger after the work transfers inside?

The measure is better ownership, stronger decisions, more executable pursuits, and a healthier transition into delivery.
