Approach

Structured around the account outcome—not the consulting activity.

Work begins with the decision, the internal owner, the evidence available, and the point at which responsibility must transfer into the enterprise.

01

Executive Assessment

A bounded assessment for an account, market, pursuit, partner route, commercial decision, or delivery risk that requires independent judgment.

  • Evidence review
  • Decision memo
  • Priority actions
02

Focused Account or Pursuit Leadership

Hands-on leadership for a defined account initiative or material pursuit, with internal teams, decision rights, governance, and transition points established at the outset.

  • Account plan
  • Capture plan
  • Team mobilization
03

Transition or Recovery Support

Short-duration intervention when commitments, delivery ownership, forecast quality, partner alignment, or client confidence need to be stabilized.

  • Risk triage
  • Recovery cadence
  • Controlled handoff

Operating Cadence

A practical sequence from evidence to ownership.

The method is deliberately compatible with enterprise account teams, capture governance, regional ownership, and delivery organizations.

01

Establish the baseline

Clarify account maturity, relationships, pipeline quality, commercial assumptions, delivery history, decision rights, and unresolved risks.

02

Choose and mobilize

Set priorities, define the account or pursuit thesis, identify the internal sponsor, assemble the cross-functional team, and allocate effort against evidence.

03

Govern the work

Use explicit milestones, customer actions, capture gates, commercial reviews, forecast updates, and executive decisions to maintain pace and quality.

04

Transfer durable ownership

Document the plan, relationships, commitments, risks, and operating cadence so mature activity can transition without losing the account strategy or executive continuity.

Enterprise-minded

Designed to strengthen the internal team, connect business lines and regions, and leave behind usable account and pursuit architecture.

Direct and accountable

Recommendations identify what should change, who owns the action, what evidence supports it, and when the decision must be revisited.

Commercially grounded

Pipeline, bookings, revenue, award value, contract ceiling, margin, cost, and risk are kept distinct rather than blended into an inflated growth story.

Conflict-aware

Work is subject to clear confidentiality, representation, intellectual-property, and conflict boundaries.

The Test

Will the organization be stronger after the work transfers inside?

The measure is not the volume of advisory activity. It is better account ownership, stronger decisions, more executable pursuits, and a healthier transition into delivery.
