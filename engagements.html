Approach

Experienced support that fits the way you work.

Sometimes a priority needs another perspective. Sometimes it needs dedicated bandwidth. Lochlann works with the people already accountable for the outcome, takes on an agreed piece of the work, and makes the handoff straightforward.

Additional experience without an extra layer between your team and the work.

Engagement modes

Different ways to add capacity.

The assignment can be as focused as a short customer or market review, or as involved as leading a pursuit through submission. We agree on outcomes, timing, and how best to work together.

01

Executive Assessment

A short, independent review of a customer, market, pursuit, or delivery question, drawing on existing work and offering practical options for your team to consider.

  • Facts and findings
  • Decision brief
  • Recommended next steps
02

Focused Growth or Pursuit Leadership

Active support for an account, new market, partner strategy, capture campaign, or proposal—leading an agreed workstream alongside your existing team.

  • Growth or capture plan
  • Cross-functional team
  • Decision schedule
03

Transition or Recovery Support

Additional program leadership and coordination during award transition, partner mobilization, startup, or a time-sensitive recovery effort.

  • Immediate risk priorities
  • Recovery plan
  • Handoff to accountable owner

Working cadence

A straightforward way to work together.

No new operating system required. I adapt to your team’s process and make progress visible through a few practical steps.

01

Get oriented.

Understand what your team has already developed, the customer and stakeholders involved, and the milestones that matter.

02

Agree on priorities.

Confirm my responsibilities, the people I will work with, and the tangible outputs you want from the assignment.

03

Work alongside the team.

Lead the agreed tasks, coordinate with stakeholders, and report progress through the rhythms your team already uses.

04

Wrap up cleanly.

Transfer the materials, relationships, decisions, and open actions to the team member taking the work forward.

Working together

A good extension of your existing team.

01

Build on what works

I start by understanding your people, customer relationships, processes, and priorities, then contribute where I can be most useful.

02

Own the agreed work

I take responsibility for clear deliverables and communication, without adding unnecessary management overhead.

03

Talk plainly about tradeoffs

When choices arise, I bring practical options and their customer, financial, schedule, and delivery implications to the people making the call.

04

Respect your boundaries

We agree on confidentiality, access, decision rights, reporting, and the planned handoff before work starts.

The intended outcome

Useful progress that stays with your team.

The aim is to move an important priority forward while leaving behind plans, materials, relationships, and next steps your team can put to work.
