Engagements

Defined structures for consequential work.

Senior attention, clear ownership, and an explicit decision or operating outcome.

Three Structures

Scope follows the decision.

Work can be focused, embedded, or transitional without becoming open-ended consulting.

01

Decision and Opportunity Assessment

A short, independent review before major investment or market commitment.

  • Executive review
  • Opportunity assessment
  • CEO / board advisory
02

Embedded Growth and Capture Leadership

Temporary senior ownership through qualification, positioning, teaming, and proposal.

  • Capture executive
  • Fractional growth leader
  • Market-entry lead
03

Transition, Execution, and Recovery

Direct leadership from award through stable startup—or intervention when performance drifts.

  • Post-award transition
  • Program startup
  • Recovery intervention
Operating Principle

Senior attention remains senior attention.

Direct: The advisor doing the work remains accountable.

Defined: Scope, decision rights, cadence, and outputs are explicit.

Selective: Engagements begin only when the consequence warrants the intervention.

Start with the decision, not the package.
