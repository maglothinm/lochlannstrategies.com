The work

Six ways to move an important opportunity forward.

The work can begin in any one area. When the issue crosses account, capture, commercial, and delivery boundaries, Lochlann keeps the decisions and owners connected.

01

Strategic Account & Portfolio Planning

Decide which accounts deserve investment, who influences the buying decision, where the customer has unmet needs, and what the account team should do next.

  • Account priorities
  • Stakeholder maps
  • Unmet needs
  • Multi-year plans
02

Prime, OEM & Partner Strategy

Choose the best route to the customer—direct, through a prime or OEM, with a teaming partner, or across business lines—and make roles, workshare, approvals, and delivery accountability clear.

  • Route to market
  • Teaming agreements
  • Partner review
  • Roles and workshare
03

Capture & Commercial Positioning

Qualify the opportunity, test the competitive position, set the win strategy, assemble the team, align price and risk, and direct gate reviews and proposal development.

  • Opportunity qualification
  • Win strategy
  • Price and risk
  • Proposal direction
04

Market & Customer Entry

Before committing people and money, test the customer need, procurement path, local constraints, partner requirements, competitive landscape, and economics of delivery.

  • Customer validation
  • Procurement path
  • Local constraints
  • Go / no-go
05

Transition & Delivery Readiness

Before pursuit commitments become delivery problems, turn them into clear scope, schedule, cost, site requirements, named owners, acceptance criteria, and a controlled handoff.

  • Capture-to-delivery
  • Site readiness
  • Implementation ownership
  • Acceptance planning
06

Growth Operations & Recovery

Improve what leadership can see and act on: pipeline quality, CRM discipline, forecasts, leadership reviews, pursuit gates, resource choices, and corrective action when activity is not producing results.

  • Pipeline quality
  • Forecast accuracy
  • Leadership reviews
  • Salesforce discipline
