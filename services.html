Selective by Design

Defined work. Clear boundaries. Selective by design.

Engagements are accepted selectively and organized around a specific decision, account, pursuit, transition, or operating problem—not as a standing substitute for internal account or delivery ownership.

Confidentiality, conflicts, representation, decision rights, and expected outcomes are established before work begins.
