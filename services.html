Advisory Areas

Support for the decisions that determine growth.

Focused senior judgment from qualification through execution.

Six Areas

Start where the decision is weakest.

Each engagement is defined around a decision, accountable owner, and usable output.

01

Executive Opportunity Assessment

Independent test of demand, competitive position, investment, win probability, and delivery exposure.

  • Pursue / no-pursue
  • Risk logic
  • Executive recommendation
02

Capture Strategy

Direct challenge of customer posture, differentiation, teaming, pricing logic, and proposal direction.

  • Win strategy
  • Teaming position
  • Decision gates
03

Market Entry

A credible route into federal, regulated, critical-infrastructure, or unfamiliar commercial markets.

  • Entry thesis
  • Partner path
  • Investment sequence
04

Fractional Growth Leadership

Recurring executive ownership of pipeline, pursuits, customer strategy, and operating cadence.

  • Growth governance
  • Pipeline discipline
  • Leadership alignment
05

Post-Award Transition

Governance, partner integration, customer alignment, PMO startup, and execution readiness.

  • 90-day plan
  • Operating model
  • Executive oversight
06

Pursuit or Program Recovery

Diagnosis and corrective action when ownership, assumptions, cadence, or confidence are failing.

  • Root cause
  • Recovery plan
  • Decision ownership
