Engagement boundaries

A clear problem, a clear owner, and a clean handoff.

01

Start with a decision.

Define what leadership needs to decide, by when, and what evidence or work product will support that decision.

02

Agree on ground rules.

Set scope, access, roles, protected information, decision rights, cadence, and limits before work begins.

03

Leave the team ready to run it.

Transfer the plan, tools, logic, and next decisions to the internal owner rather than creating an indefinite dependency.
