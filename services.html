The work

Six connected capabilities. One accountable growth system.

Each capability can stand alone, but the strongest outcomes come from preserving continuity across account strategy, capture, commercial judgment, and delivery.

01

Strategic Account & Portfolio Strategy

Prioritize accounts, map buying centers and investment pathways, identify defensible white space, and convert ambition into governed multi-year plans.

  • Account priorities
  • Stakeholder maps
  • White-space thesis
  • Portfolio cadence
02

Prime, OEM & Partner Ecosystems

Determine where direct access, teaming, subcontracting, specialist partners, or cross-enterprise collaboration create the strongest route to growth.

  • Partner strategy
  • Teaming logic
  • Due diligence
03

Capture & Commercial Positioning

Lead qualification, competitive assessment, win strategy, team formation, pricing and risk alignment, gate reviews, and proposal direction.

  • Win strategy
  • Capture governance
  • Bid investment
  • Commercial risk
04

Market & Customer Entry

Test customer demand, procurement pathways, local constraints, partner requirements, and delivery economics before market entry becomes an expensive organizational commitment.

  • Customer evidence
  • Entry pathways
  • Operating constraints
  • Go / no-go
05

Transition & Delivery Readiness

Translate pursuit commitments into scope, schedule, cost, site and facility needs, implementation ownership, acceptance criteria, and a controlled handoff.

  • Capture-to-delivery
  • Site readiness
  • Implementation risk
  • Client continuity
06

Growth Operating System & Recovery

Strengthen pipeline quality, CRM discipline, reviews, forecasting, pursuit gates, resource decisions, and recovery actions when activity is not converting.

  • Forecast integrity
  • Executive cadence
  • Salesforce discipline
