The work

Six ways to move a growth priority forward.

The work can begin in any one area. When the issue crosses market, capture, partner, commercial, and delivery boundaries, Lochlann keeps the decisions and owners connected.

01

Growth Strategy & Portfolio Choices

Decide which markets, customers, and opportunities justify investment by testing customer need, competitive position, revenue potential, timing, and delivery economics.

  • Market priorities
  • Customer segmentation
  • Investment cases
  • Multi-year growth plans
02

Partner & Route-to-Market Strategy

Choose the best route to market—direct, through a prime, channel, technology partner, or local teammate—and make roles, incentives, workshare, approvals, and delivery accountability clear.

  • Route to market
  • Teaming and channels
  • Partner due diligence
  • Roles and workshare
03

Capture & Proposal Leadership

Qualify the opportunity, test the competitive position, set the win strategy, assemble the team, align price and risk, and direct gate reviews and proposal development.

  • Opportunity qualification
  • Win strategy
  • Price and risk
  • Proposal direction
04

Market & Customer Entry

Before committing people and money, test the customer need, procurement path, local constraints, partner requirements, competitive landscape, and economics of delivery.

  • Customer validation
  • Procurement path
  • Local constraints
  • Go / no-go
05

Transition & Execution Readiness

Turn pursuit commitments into clear scope, schedule, cost, technical and site requirements, named owners, acceptance criteria, and a controlled handoff.

  • Strategy-to-execution
  • Operational readiness
  • Implementation ownership
  • Acceptance planning
06

Growth Operations & Recovery

Improve what leadership can see and act on: pipeline quality, CRM discipline, forecasts, leadership reviews, pursuit gates, resource choices, and corrective action when activity is not producing results.

  • Pipeline quality
  • Forecast accuracy
  • Leadership reviews
  • Salesforce discipline
