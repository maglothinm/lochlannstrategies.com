Michael Maglothin · Founder & Principal

Built from the perspective of the buyer, prime, partner, growth leader, and program operator.

Michael J. Maglothin founded Lochlann Strategies as an independent executive platform for strategic accounts, major pursuits, partner ecosystems, and delivery-intensive growth.

His work has spanned U.S. Air Force requirements and acquisition, prime-contractor growth, multinational capture, strategic teaming, global sales operations, program startup, and international delivery.

The recurring assignment has been to turn complex customer needs into an account strategy, align the enterprise around a credible path to win, and ensure that commercial commitments survive contact with implementation.

Enterprise orientation

Selective outside perspective—structured to complement enterprise ownership.

Lochlann is intentionally designed for discrete, bounded work and professional dialogue. It is not a volume consulting practice.

Operating convictions

Discipline before activity.

01

Judgment before activity

More meetings, proposals, partners, or pipeline do not automatically create more value. Evidence and disciplined choices come first.

02

Ownership before ambiguity

Accounts and pursuits need an executive sponsor, an operating owner, explicit decision rights, and a defined path to delivery ownership.

03

Commercial quality before volume

Bookings, revenue, margin, cash, delivery risk, and client confidence matter more than unsupported total contract value.

04

Durability before dependence

The work should leave stronger account plans, teams, relationships, governance, and execution pathways inside the organization.
