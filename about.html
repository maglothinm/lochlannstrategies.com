The career behind the work

He has defined requirements, led pursuits, built partner teams, and carried programs into delivery.

Michael founded Lochlann Strategies to bring that full-lifecycle judgment directly to leadership teams facing an important account, major pursuit, or difficult transition. He remains the hands-on lead from the first assessment through the handoff.

His career has included serving as the government customer within the U.S. Air Force; leading growth, capture, and teaming for prime integrators; and directing program startup and international delivery.

That range lets him test an opportunity from more than one angle: Is the need real? Is the path to win credible? Can the organization stand behind its promise? Who owns the next decision?

Why the full lifecycle matters

Internal ownership may be divided. Customer confidence is not.

Account strategy, capture, pricing, teaming, and delivery are often managed by different groups. The customer sees one organization and judges one promise.

Lochlann brings the people who must approve, price, build, and deliver the work into the conversation early, so the pursuit reflects real technical, commercial, schedule, site, and delivery constraints.

Working principles

A few rules keep the work useful.

01

Evidence before activity

More meetings, partners, proposals, and pipeline do not make an opportunity better. The facts should determine where time and money go.

02

One owner for each decision

Every account and pursuit needs an executive sponsor, a day-to-day owner, clear decision rights, and a named delivery lead before the handoff.

03

Quality over headline value

Revenue matters, but so do margin, cash, delivery risk, and customer confidence. Total contract value alone is not a strategy.

04

Build internal ownership

The engagement should leave stronger plans, clearer roles, better relationships, and an internal team that can carry the work forward.
