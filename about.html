The career behind the work

I’ve worked on the customer side, the pursuit team, and the delivery team.

I started Lochlann so organizations can draw on that experience for a particular opportunity, customer, or period of high demand, without creating a permanent role around it. When we work together, I’m the person doing the work.

I’ve served as the government customer in the U.S. Air Force, helped build companies and markets, led federal and international capture and growth efforts, and managed complex programs through implementation and acceptance.

My first priority is to understand how your team works and where I can contribute. That may be targeted customer insight, dedicated capture capacity, a partner strategy, or help through a difficult transition. Your team keeps the direction; I take responsibility for the part we agree I can help with.
