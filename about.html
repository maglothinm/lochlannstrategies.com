Michael Maglothin · Founder & Principal

Built from the perspective of the buyer, prime, partner, growth leader, and program operator.

Michael established Lochlann Strategies as a focused platform for selective advisory, thought leadership, and professional continuity, drawing on a career inside government and enterprise organizations.

His work has spanned U.S. Air Force requirements and acquisition, prime-contractor growth, multinational capture, strategic teaming, global sales operations, program startup, and international delivery.

The recurring assignment has been to turn complex customer needs into an account strategy, align the enterprise around a credible path to win, and ensure that commercial commitments can survive contact with implementation.

Enterprise Orientation

Selective outside perspective—structured to complement enterprise ownership.

Lochlann is intentionally structured for discrete, bounded work and professional dialogue. It is not designed as a volume consulting practice.

The strongest outcomes come from mobilizing multidisciplinary enterprise teams, aligning regional and functional owners, and keeping accountability connected through delivery.

Judgment before activity

More meetings, proposals, partners, or pipeline do not automatically create more value. Evidence and disciplined choices come first.

Ownership before ambiguity

Accounts and pursuits require an executive sponsor, an operating owner, explicit decision rights, and a defined path to regional or delivery ownership.

Commercial quality before volume

Bookings, revenue, margin, cash, delivery risk, and client confidence matter more than unsupported total contract value.

Durability before dependence

The work should leave stronger account plans, teams, relationships, governance, and execution pathways inside the organization.

Professional Dialogue

Discuss the account, market, pursuit, or operating decision that carries consequence.

Conversations are selective, confidential, and grounded in the evidence available.
