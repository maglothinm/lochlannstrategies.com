Full account lifecycle

Five roles, from customer need through delivery.

The organizations and titles changed, but the same decisions recurred across those roles: understand the customer, shape a credible solution, assemble the team, win the work, and deliver what was promised.

Customer Pursuit Delivery
01

Government customer

Requirements & acquisition

In the U.S. Air Force, Michael led requirements and Pentagon liaison work for force-protection modernization. His acquisition work also included RFPs, statements of work, site requirements, systems engineering, and design reviews.

02

Prime contractor

Strategic accounts & market development

In industry, he led business development across U.S. defense, other federal, and international markets, turning customer priorities into account plans and routes to market with major primes and partners.

03

Capture leader

Pursuit & partner governance

He qualified opportunities, tracked emerging requirements, built partner teams and formal teaming agreements, and took pursuits through competitive strategy, bid investment, pricing, risk, reviews, and executive approval.

04

Systems integrator

Mission systems & the built environment

His programs connected technology with the physical setting around it—from radar and security sites to ports, command centers, data centers, nuclear facilities, civil works, communications, installation, testing, and acceptance.

05

Program leader

Startup, recovery & international delivery

Michael led cross-functional teams through program startup, site readiness, implementation, recovery, testing, acceptance, and handoff, while coordinating with government customers and aligning partner, engineering, commercial, operations, and field teams across countries.
