Selected prior-career evidence

Responsibility, contribution, and measurable results.

Three examples from previous roles show the scale of responsibility and the kind of work Michael can bring to an assignment. They are not Lochlann client cases.

01

Global sales operations

Improved the capacity and economics of global bidding.

Responsibility: Led 14 direct reports across five regions supporting $1B+ in annual opportunities and $1.5B+ in proposals and quotations. Rebuilt Salesforce and integrated CRM with ERP while improving bid processes.

Measured results: Monthly bid throughput rose from four to 20, quotation labor fell 50%, and annual costs declined by more than $200,000.

Relevant to: teams scaling pursuit activity without proportionately increasing overhead.

02

Federal growth & capture

Managed complex federal pursuits and investment decisions.

Responsibility: Governed a strategic pipeline above $600M, a $50M annual factored pipeline, and a $2M bid-and-proposal budget. Led qualification, teaming, competitive strategy, pricing coordination, proposals, reviews, and transitions.

Relevant to: organizations that need accountable leadership and sound judgment across high-stakes bid decisions.

03

International growth & delivery

Connected international pursuits to field execution.

Responsibility: Aligned market development, technical solutions, partners, and delivery across international programs as annual business grew from approximately $3M to more than $50M. Contributed as a sales-technical and program-management bid lead to a $150M award.

Execution experience: Led programs in multiple countries, spanning the transition from commercial commitments into practical delivery.

Relevant to: government and mission-technology pursuits where partner strategy and operational delivery must work together.
